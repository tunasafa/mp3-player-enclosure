# Engraving artwork

The geometric bluefin silhouette was drawn as original vector contours for this device. `make_branding.py` generates `branding.json` and the two SVGs. Lettering is outlined DejaVu Sans; its license accompanies the artwork. No font installation is required to rebuild the CAD from the saved contours.

SVGs use face-view coordinates in millimetres inside the 64 × 128 mm exterior. The CAD mirrors X for the front face because the front is viewed along +Z, and leaves rear face coordinates unchanged. Engraving depth is 0.03 mm. The dark floor in the viewer is a non-overlapping subdivision of the actual engraved steel shape, not a floating decal. Steel faces retain 0.37 mm nominal material below their engraving.

Back identification is design information, not certification or a fabricated production serial number. The metal finish shown is a satin polished rendering target; surface finish samples and laser-mark contrast must be approved with the fabricator.
