| Connection | Recommended approach | What must be settled |
|---|---|---|
| Screen → interface | Plug the supplied LCD and touch tails directly into their respective ZIFs. | LCD **45P/0.5 mm** and touch **8P/0.5 mm** are supplier-listed. Obtain pinouts, contact faces, stiffener thickness, bend limits and actual tail reach. Extra extension cables are not automatically needed. |
| XIAO → interface | 0.5 mm-pitch FFC through an adapter attached to the XIAO pads. | All 11 edge GPIOs are allocated in the earlier proposed scheme, leaving control/reset signals to a GPIO expander or a revised verified allocation. Final interface/power circuitry and pin mapping remain to design. |
| XIAO → DAC | Short FFC between dedicated adapters. | Carry I2S DIN/BCK/WSEL, I2C SDA/SCL, reset, power and adequate grounds. Separate MCLK can be omitted only with the DAC configured to derive its clock from BCLK. Final conductor count is not selected. |
| Controller/interface → microSD | Short FFC into a selected low-profile SD PCB. | SPI SCK/MOSI/MISO, separate SD CS, power and sufficient grounds. Shared LCD/SD SPI requires independent chip-selects and compatible firmware bus ownership. |
| Battery → power circuitry | Precrimped short flexible wire pair with a polarized locking connector. | Peak current, conductor rating, polarity, connector clearance and strain relief. Ordinary narrow signal FFC is not an approved battery harness. |

An example low-profile connector family for **new inter-board adapters** is Hirose FH19C/FH19SC: 0.5 mm pitch and about 0.9 mm height. Choose the exact member by contact count, bottom-contact orientation and supported cable thickness. This family is not a selection for the display's 45-pin connector. The JST-GH power connector candidate is 1.25 mm pitch with a latch; its published 1 A rating is specified with AWG26, not a blanket rating for every thinner cable sold online.

Do not select cables only by pitch. Match conductor count, same-face/opposite-face contacts, pin-1 mapping, insertion thickness, exposed contact length, stiffener, latch access, bend requirements and current rating. Seller A/B cable labels are insufficient without an end-contact drawing. Cable length is measured along the actual folded route with service slack, not straight-line board distance. Avoid stacking connectors/ribbons over the DAC: the metal design has only 0.18 mm nominal rear dielectric clearance there.

## Electrical checks still needed

- **Battery-only operation:** Seeed documents no output on the XIAO 5 V pin when running on battery. Do not build the portable system around that pin as a battery-fed 5 V source.
- **Headphone audio:** Adafruit documents headphone operation with a low-voltage supply and 3.3 V logic, I2C configuration, and required reset. Its 2-pin JST-PH socket is **speaker output**, not battery input or a digital-audio connector. No additional headphone amplifier or jack is needed for the selected board.
- **Backlight:** Startek lists four parallel LEDs at approximately 3.2 V and 80 mA total. A current-regulated driver and brightness control need selection after the exact module circuit is known; powering the whole module at 3.3 V does not independently regulate its backlight.
- **Power budget:** Verify the selected XIAO revision's regulator/charger, combined ESP32/display/SD/DAC peak load, charging while playing, termination, temperature, and safe startup/off/wake. A charge-management chip alone does not qualify the system power path.
- **Battery indication/control:** The current files have not completed battery sensing, a user-accessible hard switch, or touch wake. Any fuel gauge, extra ADC, power switch or power-management part depends on that completed design; no separate module is presently approved as fitting.
- **Physical fit:** Measure actual solder, adapter boards, latches and wires. Preserve both battery expansion reserves. A successful mechanical CAD check does not establish electrical function or actual purchased-part fit.
