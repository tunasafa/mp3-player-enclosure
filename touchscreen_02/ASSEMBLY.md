# M02-02 assembly sequence

All coordinates and parts refer to `designs/M02_02/assembly_NOMINAL.step`. Work with the front face down on a clean, flat, non-scratching support; Z increases toward the open rear. This is the sequence for a measured-parts prototype, not a validated production work instruction.

1. **Inspect the parts.** Measure the finished DAC including solder and leads, the complete LCD/CTP and flexes, the approved battery assembly and the selected SD/interface boards. Compare against `measurements.csv` and the critical gaps in `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`. Check metal flatness, remove burrs and clean the bonding surfaces. Do not use a damaged or swollen pouch.
2. **Bond the front face to the frame.** Fit the 0.15 mm front perimeter bond. Keep adhesive out of the screen window, screw pilots, flex relief and all port openings. Cure on a flat support using the adhesive supplier's process.
3. **Fit the display.** Apply the continuous 0.15 mm display adhesive to the front lip. Lower the complete display through the rear opening. The maximum LCD outline has only 0.27 mm nominal clearance per side; never force the glass. Confirm the active image is centred in the window before committing the bond.
4. **Install the XIAO.** Fit its 0.35 mm insulating adhesive pad. Keep the removable insertion stop out. Start 1.6 mm inward from its final X position, lower to its mounting plane, then slide toward the right until the USB mouth reaches X31.60. This path is checked against the frame. Seat the board on its pad and fit the separate stop and its 0.15 mm adhesive. Batteries and wiring must not occupy this temporary installation space yet.
5. **Install the DAC.** Fit the 0.15 mm insulating pad; keep the removable stop out. Start 2.0 mm above its final Y position, lower the intact board, then slide toward the bottom until the original jack mouth reaches Y−63.60. Fit the separate top stop and its adhesive. Do not bend the jack or detach it from the PCB.
6. **Prepare the display carrier.** Bond the two stiffening ribs and the SD edge stops with their specified finite bond layers. Set the removable SD insertion stop aside until the SD board has been seated. Fit the LCD perimeter cushion and carrier; route the actual display/touch flex through the side relief and carrier opening. The modeled routing spaces are provisional and must be checked against the sample's permitted bend radius.
7. **Fasten the carrier.** Use four nominal M1.4 × 3 screws. The holes lie outside the glass outline; the heads must not bear on the LCD. Validate the actual screw pilot and clamp load on a coupon before using the display assembly. No tightening torque has been qualified.
8. **Install the upper boards.** The SD board sits on its 0.30 mm insulating pad. Start 1.6 mm inward from the left wall, lower, slide left and seat it, then bond the rear insertion stop. Insert the SD card through the port after the socket board is fixed. Mount the interface board on its two insulating lands; connect the qualified 45-pin display and touch connectors without stretching the tails. Retain the latch-access area.
9. **Install the battery assembly.** Seat the two approved pack sections on their separate 0.30 mm insulating adhesive pads. The rails have nominal lateral clearance; they are guides, not clamps. Keep the 1.70 mm rear expansion volumes empty. No rigid board, strap, screw, harness or rear foam may enter them.
10. **Route and secure the wiring.** Place the pack's approved protection/lead joint in its allocation. Use the reserved lead passages through the rails. Add strain relief at the board and pack exits. The final harness, wire gauges, solder heights and connector bodies must be checked in the assembled device; routing reservations are not a finished harness. Bench-test charging, display/touch, audio and SD before closing the case.
11. **Prepare the rear assembly.** Bond the three nonconductive DAC spacers to the rear face with their 0.10 mm adhesive disks. Their locating pins enter the actual DAC holes; their shoulders seat on the PCB annuli. Fit the 0.07 mm dielectric liner with its cutouts around these spacers and case fasteners. Keep adhesive clear of every pilot and the battery expansion spaces.
12. **Close the enclosure.** Fit the 0.15 mm installed rear gasket and lower the rear face so the DAC locating pins engage without force. The raised frame lands provide hard stops at Z7.90, defining the 0.15 mm installed perimeter-gasket gap. Install the seven nominal M1.6 × 5 countersunk screws gradually in an alternating sequence. Verify that the rear face stays flat and the pouches remain uncompressed. Qualify pilot sizing, screw length and tightening torque experimentally.
13. **Fit and test the covers.** Install the prototype flush USB, SD and jack covers. Verify they remain removable, retain securely and do not contact electrical terminals. Test their compression/retention and the complete enclosure for the intended ingress level before making any water-resistance claim. Remove the appropriate cover before inserting a cable, card or headphones.

For service, disconnect USB power, remove covers and rear screws, lift the rear assembly without pulling on the DAC, then disconnect the pack using its approved service connection. Follow the battery adhesive supplier's removal process; do not lever against or puncture a pouch. The approved pack's service connector and disconnect details are a release gate.
